How QCatalyst Works

1
Select a Reaction

Pick the reaction you want to optimise — HER, ORR, CO₂RR, OER, or NH₃ synthesis. Each has a key thermodynamic descriptor that determines which catalysts will be best.

2
Choose a Mode

Fast Surrogate: A machine-learning model predicts adsorption energies instantly from material properties (d-band centre, electronegativity, atomic radius).

Quantum VQE: Runs a real Variational Quantum Eigensolver circuit on a simulated quantum computer (Qiskit Aer). Slower but demonstrates the quantum workflow.

3
Adjust Weights & Run

Use the sliders to decide how much you care about activity, stability, selectivity, cost, and abundance. Then click Run Screening.

4
Read the Results

Leaderboard: All 31 materials ranked by composite score.
Volcano Plot: Hover over dots — best catalysts sit at the peak. The green star is the theoretical optimum.
Radar Chart: Top-3 compared across all criteria.
Click any row to see the energy profile, VQE convergence, 3D viewer, and properties.

5
Export

Click Download CSV to save the full ranked results for further analysis.

⚠ Important: All results are simplified approximations. The VQE uses a minimal H₂ proxy model, not a real metal surface. Results are NOT experimentally validated.
